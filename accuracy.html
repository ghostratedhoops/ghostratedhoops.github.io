How accurate is Ghost Rating?

Two numbers, one test, and what it doesn't show.

TWO NUMBERS

Ghost Value is what a player is worth compared with an average player of any role. It is the engine: every part of the game, weighted by five seasons of real results.

Ghost Rating, the headline number, is Ghost Value compared with an average player in the same role (same position, usage and three-point rate). It answers a different question: not who helps most, but who is best at the job he's given.

Taking out what a role normally produces also takes out real value, so Ghost Rating isn't built to predict games. We test it anyway and show both.

THE TEST

For each season from 2022-23 to 2025-26:

  1. Fit the weights (and the role averages) using only earlier seasons.
  2. Rate every player from his two previous seasons. Players with no history count as a below-average newcomer (−2).
  3. For every game, add up the ratings of the players who actually played, weighted by how long they were on the floor, and compare the two teams.
  4. Check that prediction against the real final margin.

Every method goes through the exact same steps. Only the player ratings change, and all are put on the same scale so no method gets an edge from its units.

HOW SURE ARE WE?

We reshuffled the 4,912 games 2,000 times and recomputed each gap.

Ghost Value

  • vs box score only: ahead by 0.029 (90% range 0.019 to 0.039); ahead in 100% of 2,000 reshuffles
  • vs adjusted plus-minus (rapm): ahead by 0.025 (90% range 0.017 to 0.033); ahead in 100% of 2,000 reshuffles
  • vs raw plus-minus: ahead by 0.059 (90% range 0.049 to 0.069); ahead in 100% of 2,000 reshuffles
  • vs points per 100: ahead by 0.078 (90% range 0.062 to 0.093); ahead in 100% of 2,000 reshuffles

Ghost Rating

  • vs box score only: ahead by 0.008 (90% range -0.004 to 0.020); ahead in 88% of 2,000 reshuffles
  • vs adjusted plus-minus (rapm): ahead by 0.004 (90% range -0.005 to 0.013); ahead in 75% of 2,000 reshuffles
  • vs raw plus-minus: ahead by 0.038 (90% range 0.028 to 0.048); ahead in 100% of 2,000 reshuffles
  • vs points per 100: ahead by 0.057 (90% range 0.038 to 0.074); ahead in 100% of 2,000 reshuffles

Ghost Value's edge over box-score ratings is small, about 0.03, but it showed up every time. Ghost Rating's edge over box-score ratings and adjusted plus-minus is too small to call: we'd say they're even.

WHAT THIS DOESN'T SHOW

  • We haven't compared against other public all-in-one stats (EPM, LEBRON, DARKO, BPM). We'd need their historical numbers to run the same test fairly.
  • The test knows who played and for how long. It checks how well the ratings value players, not how well they forecast a game before tip-off.
  • Whether a player is good "for his role" can't be checked against game results directly. The test only shows that the role version still tracks winning.
  • Rookies and players with little history are all treated the same, which hurts every method equally.
